# Phrase Bank

Use these as patterns, not as fixed text.

## Opening the Problem

- `[Paradigm] has gained substantial attention in [domain] due to its ability to [capability].`
- `However, [specific bottleneck] makes it impractical for [real use case].`
- `For instance, [numbered example] restricts [metric] to [value], well below [requirement].`
- `Despite this necessity, [subproblem] remains underexplored.`

## Positioning Prior Work

- `Existing methods typically rely on [assumption], which fails to [requirement].`
- `Although [method family] can [benefit], it requires [cost] and therefore [limitation].`
- `These methods are inherently mismatched with [dynamics/setting] because [reason].`
- `This limitation motivates a [granularity/adaptation/efficiency] mechanism.`

## Introducing Observations

- `To examine this limitation, we [perform analysis].`
- `As shown in Figure X, [observation].`
- `This suggests that [design implication].`
- `Motivated by this observation, we propose [method].`

## Introducing the Method

- `To address this, we propose [method], a [role] for [goal].`
- `[Method] discards [old paradigm] and instead [new paradigm].`
- `The key to [method] lies in [core mechanism].`
- `To operationalize this insight, we design [module].`

## Handling Multiple Bottlenecks

- `However, this paradigm raises two primary bottlenecks: (1) [bottleneck 1], and (2) [bottleneck 2].`
- `To address the first bottleneck, we design [module].`
- `To overcome the second bottleneck, we introduce [module].`
- `Together, these designs enable [result] while [constraint].`

## Method Details

- `We first formulate [process] as [levels/units].`
- `A naive solution would [do X]. However, [why it fails].`
- `Instead of [expensive operation], [module] learns to [prediction/action].`
- `Concretely, [definition].`
- `Owing to these designs, [method] [benefit].`

## Experiment Interpretation

- `We compare [method] against [baselines] across [benchmarks].`
- `As shown in Table X, [method] achieves [result] while [quality constraint].`
- `The advantage is most pronounced on [hard setting], where [baseline failure] but [method behavior].`
- `We attribute this improvement to [module/mechanism].`
- `These results verify that [claim].`

## Contribution Bullets

- `We introduce [method], a [property] method for [problem].`
- `We identify [observation/failure mode] and design [module] to [effect].`
- `We develop [module], which [mechanism] by [technical route].`
- `We conduct extensive experiments on [scope], demonstrating [headline result].`

## Captions

- `Figure X. [Takeaway]. [Setting]. [Observation]. [Implication].`
- `Table X. [Benchmark setting]. [Metrics]. [Main result].`

## Tightening Vague Sentences

Before:

`Our method improves performance and efficiency.`

After:

`Our method reduces [cost metric] by [number] while maintaining [quality metric] on [benchmark].`

Before:

`Existing methods cannot handle dynamics.`

After:

`Existing static schedules are fixed before rollout and therefore cannot adjust to observation-dependent sparsity patterns during robot-environment interaction.`

Before:

`We use a module to solve this problem.`

After:

`To avoid [failure mode], [module] [mechanism], enabling [benefit].`
